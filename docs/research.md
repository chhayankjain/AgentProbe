# Research

AgentProbe began as the toolkit behind the paper *"AgentProbe: Systematic Fault Injection and Observability for Evaluating the Reliability of LLM-Based Intelligent Agents"* (submitted to MAKE, MDPI, 2026).

The benchmark harness, the failure taxonomy, every experiment batch, the paper source and the steps to reproduce its results are in the [`research/` folder on GitHub](https://github.com/chhayankjain/agentprobe-toolkit/tree/main/research).
